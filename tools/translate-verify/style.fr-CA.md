Write Canadian French (fr-CA) in the register of the official French edition.

**Register**

- **Narration** keeps the official tense and voice: literary first person, with the *passé simple*
  in past narration where the official French uses it. Keep the narrator's mood: awe stays awe,
  dread stays dread.
- **Instructions to players** use *vous* in the imperative, as on the rulebook's cards:
  « Gagnez 3 PV. », « Défaussez une carte. ». Rules must say exactly what the English says: the same
  amounts, conditions, targets and order.
- **Buttons and short labels** use the infinitive, as in the app's interface: « Continuer »,
  « Accepter », « Fermer ».
- Game terms follow the rulebook, *Le Secret de mon père* (Origames), including its capitals:
  Domaine, Génération, Expérience, Grand Œuvre.
- Running text names the game and its scenarios by the French edition's titles. The app itself is
  still called *My Father's Work*.

**Typography** (the checks normalize it, but write it this way)

- No space before `?`, `!` or `;`.
- A non-breaking space before `:`.
- Guillemets « » with a non-breaking space inside them.
- Amounts: « 2 $ », « 3 PV ».
- Write the ligatures: Œ, œ.
